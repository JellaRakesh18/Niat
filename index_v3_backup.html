Mazdoor Safety & Rights Guidelines

Confirm Wages Upfront: Always agree on daily wage and working hours before travelling to the work site.
Zero Registration Fees: Mazdoor Mitra is 100% free. Never pay any advance fee or deposit to get a job.
Safety Equipment (PPE): Employers must provide helmets, boots, and harnesses for elevated or hazardous work.
Attendance & Proof: Maintain a daily diary or WhatsApp photos of completed work as proof of attendance.
