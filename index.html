Visual First-Aid Quick Guides

Field Procedures

1. Heat Exhaustion & Dehydration

Move worker to shade immediately. Loosen tight clothing. Apply cold wet cloth on forehead and neck. Provide ORS (Oral Rehydration Solution) or salted lime water in sips. Do not force large gulps.

2. Electric Shock & High Voltage

DO NOT TOUCH the victim with bare hands. Immediately switch off the main circuit or use dry wood/bamboo to separate victim from wire. Check pulse and breathing; begin CPR if trained. Call 108 immediately.

3. Heavy Bleeding & Fractures

Apply firm direct pressure over wound using clean cloth or bandage. Elevate injured limb above heart if no fracture. For broken bones, keep limb completely still with cardboard/wood splint.

4. Cement Dust & Chemical Eye Burns

Do not rub the eye. Flush continuously with clean, gentle flowing drinking water for at least 15 minutes holding eyelids wide open. Cover with sterile pad and transport to eye specialist.
